---
question: |-
  Your company plans to manage its Azure resources as groups that share the same lifecycle.

  Which Azure construct should you use to group the resources?
documentation:
  - title: "Azure Resource Manager overview"
    url: "https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-resource-manager/management/overview"
skill_area: "resource-management"
study_guide: "2026-01-01"
archetype: "fact-recall"
---

- [x] A resource group
  > A resource group holds related resources that you deploy, update and delete together.
- [ ] A management group
  > Management groups organize subscriptions for policy and access; they don't hold resources directly.
- [ ] A tag
  > Tags add metadata for filtering and cost reports; they don't control a lifecycle.
